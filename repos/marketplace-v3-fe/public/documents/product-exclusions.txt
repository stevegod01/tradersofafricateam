TRADERS OF AFRICA MARKETPLACE
PRODUCT EXCLUSIONS POLICY — STARTER DRAFT

Effective date: August 2026

1. PURPOSE
This starter policy identifies products that may not be listed or traded through the Traders of Africa marketplace. It should be reviewed and approved by qualified legal and compliance professionals before production use.

2. ILLEGAL AND REGULATED GOODS
Products prohibited by applicable local, national, regional, or international law are excluded. Regulated goods may only be listed where the marketplace expressly permits them and the supplier has provided all required licenses.

3. COUNTERFEIT AND INFRINGING PRODUCTS
Counterfeit, pirated, stolen, fraudulently labeled, or unauthorized products are prohibited. Listings must not infringe trademarks, copyrights, patents, design rights, or other intellectual-property rights.

4. DANGEROUS MATERIALS
Weapons, ammunition, explosives, toxic substances, radioactive materials, and improperly handled hazardous chemicals are prohibited unless expressly approved under a dedicated regulated-goods process.

5. UNSAFE FOOD AND AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS
Adulterated, contaminated, expired, recalled, illegally harvested, or improperly stored food and agricultural products are prohibited. Products that fail applicable phytosanitary, veterinary, labeling, or food-safety requirements are also excluded.

6. PROTECTED WILDLIFE AND ENVIRONMENTAL GOODS
Products derived from protected or endangered species, illegal logging, unlawful mining, or other environmentally prohibited activity may not be listed.

7. MEDICAL AND CONTROLLED PRODUCTS
Unapproved medicines, controlled substances, prescription products sold without authorization, unsafe supplements, and unlicensed medical devices are prohibited.

8. EXPLOITATIVE OR OFFENSIVE GOODS
Products connected to forced labor, child labor, human trafficking, hate activity, sexual exploitation, or severe human-rights abuses are prohibited.

9. RECALLS AND REMOVAL
Traders of Africa may remove any listing that creates legal, safety, reputational, or marketplace risk. Suppliers must cooperate with recalls, buyer notifications, investigations, and corrective action.

10. SUPPLIER RESPONSIBILITY
Suppliers are responsible for confirming that products are lawful in both origin and destination markets. Absence from this summary does not mean a product is automatically permitted.
