TRADERS OF AFRICA MARKETPLACE
SUPPLIER COMPLIANCE POLICY — STARTER DRAFT

Effective date: August 2026

1. PURPOSE
This starter policy outlines the minimum standards expected from every supplier using the Traders of Africa marketplace. It should be reviewed and approved by qualified legal and compliance professionals before production use.

2. SUPPLIER VERIFICATION
Suppliers must provide accurate business registration, ownership, contact, banking, tax, and identity information. Requested certificates and permits must remain current throughout the supplier relationship.

3. PRODUCT INFORMATION
Every listing must accurately describe the product, origin, quantity, condition, specification, certification, price, and delivery terms. Misleading descriptions, manipulated images, and omitted material facts are prohibited.

4. QUALITY AND SAFETY
Products must comply with applicable health, safety, agricultural, manufacturing, packaging, labeling, and export requirements in the origin and destination markets. Suppliers must maintain records that demonstrate compliance.

5. FAIR TRADING
Suppliers must not engage in fraud, bribery, price manipulation, counterfeit trade, discrimination, harassment, or deceptive sales practices. Communication with buyers must remain professional and truthful.

6. ORDER FULFILMENT
Suppliers must honor confirmed orders, agreed specifications, delivery schedules, and return or dispute obligations. Any anticipated delay or supply issue must be disclosed promptly.

7. DATA AND PRIVACY
Buyer information may only be used to complete legitimate marketplace transactions. Suppliers must protect personal and commercial data from unauthorized use or disclosure.

8. AUDITS AND DOCUMENTATION
Traders of Africa may request supporting records or conduct reasonable compliance reviews. Suppliers must cooperate and correct identified issues within the stated timeframe.

9. ENFORCEMENT
Breaches may result in listing removal, withheld transactions, account suspension, termination, reporting to relevant authorities, or other remedies allowed by the marketplace agreement and applicable law.

10. CONTACT
Questions and compliance reports should be submitted through the official Traders of Africa support channel.

DISCLAIMER
This document is an editable starter draft and does not constitute legal advice.
